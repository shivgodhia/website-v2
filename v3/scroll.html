Ghost Fighter An iPhone fighting game where you fight copies of your friends

The idea How it plays So far

Train a ghost of yourself. Dare your friends to beat it.

Your friends don't need to be online. Their ghost fights the way they do.

Your ghost learns from every match you win

Each win teaches your ghost a little more of how you fight, shown as its generation. Moves and combos are swipes, with haptics and an announcer.

Send a link that says "beat my ghost"

Friends play a best of three against your ghost, and you see how many tried and how many won. Every match can be replayed exactly.

Margin A writing app for the Mac with an AI editor

The problem What it does So far

AI writing tools rewrite you. Margin reads your edits instead.

Think Cursor, but for prose: fiction, essays and blog posts.

It suggests. You decide.

The AI editor, Muse, never changes your text. It proposes edits you accept or reject, and replies in comment threads pinned to your words.

It only reads what changed

Muse reads the document once, then only sees the diff of each edit, so it costs less the less you change. Every document has its own history, with your edits and the AI's kept apart.

Haze How Ah? Singapore's air quality, answered in Singlish

The problem What it does So far

Haze season. Can go outside anot?

The official readings are tables of numbers. Most people just want to know whether to go for that run.

One answer, from "No problem." to "Siao liao."

It reads NEA's live PM2.5 levels and gives the official advice in one line. The whole page is a sky that greys as the haze gets worse, with a map of all five regions.

A ping when your side of the island gets hazy

The Telegram bot alerts you when your region crosses a haze level, and waits for two clean readings before saying it's clear, so it doesn't flip-flop. Works in group chats too.

  • 10minutes between checks
  • 2languages, English and Chinese

Grove One command to give every AI agent its own workspace

The problem What it does So far

Running agents in parallel means a dozen worktrees by hand.

A feature that touches three repos needs three matching branches, three checkouts and a terminal for each, every time.

One command sets up every repo

Grove creates a worktree in each repo on the same branch, opens one tmux session and starts your agent. If any step fails, it rolls everything back.

A dashboard of everything in flight

An fzf dashboard lists every workspace with its pull request status, and each workspace inherits the Claude skills of the repos inside it.

  • 30test files
