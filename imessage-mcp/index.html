GitHub Download

Claude can text people for you. Only once you say yes.

A free Mac app that connects Claude to iMessage. Claude reads the conversations you allow and writes your replies. Every text waits for you to approve it before it sends.

Download for Mac See the code on GitHub

Works with Claude, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other MCP apps, on macOS 12 or later.

Three steps, about a minute

  1. Download it and drag it to Applications

    It is about 1 MB, signed and checked by Apple.

  2. One switch left Open Settings

    Open it once

    It connects itself to Claude and the other AI apps it finds, and tells you what is left.

  3. iMessage MCP

    Flip one switch

    Turn on Full Disk Access so it can read your messages, then restart Claude.

Download for Mac

Before you trust it with your texts

It runs on your Mac
There is nothing to sign up for and it has no server of its own. It reads Messages on your Mac and hands Claude only the conversations Claude asks for.
You decide what Claude sees
List the conversations Claude may read. Everything else stays invisible to it.
You approve what goes out
Claude shows you the recipient and the exact words and asks before it sends, the same way it asks for any other connector. You can also switch sending off completely.
Group chats work too
Claude reads and replies in your groups, named or not. Ask it to text a few people who aren't in a group yet and Messages opens the new group with your text in it. Switch on Accessibility and it sends once you approve; otherwise you press Return.
The code is open
About 2,300 lines of Swift under the MIT license. Read it on GitHub.
What it can't do yet
Send photos, react, or mark messages as read. It works on Mac only, and Claude checks your texts when you ask it to.
