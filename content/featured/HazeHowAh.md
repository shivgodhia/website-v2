A Singlish answer to "can go outside anot?" during Singapore's haze season. Haze How Ah? turns NEA's live PM2.5 and PSI readings into a one-line verdict, a sky that changes colour with the air, and a map of all five regions, in English and Chinese.

[@HazeHowAhSGBot](https://t.me/HazeHowAhSGBot) on Telegram sends free alerts when your side of the island crosses a haze level, and replies to /now with a shareable card. Alerts run on Supabase pg_cron every 10 minutes.
